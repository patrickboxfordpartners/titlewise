# TITLEwise

> AI-powered tools for real estate closing attorneys. Automate document review, wire fraud screening, closing checklists, and client communication.

## What is TITLEwise?

TITLEwise is a SaaS platform that gives real estate closing attorneys AI-powered tools to manage their practice more efficiently. It handles title analysis, closing disclosure review, wire fraud screening, HOA document review, fee estimation, tax proration, and automated status updates to all transaction parties.

## Who is it for?

- Solo real estate closing attorneys
- Small title company firms (2-10 attorneys)
- Any attorney handling residential real estate closings

## What problems does it solve?

- Manual document review is slow and error-prone
- Wire fraud verification requires cross-referencing multiple sources
- Status updates to buyers, sellers, agents, and lenders are repetitive
- Closing checklists are tracked in spreadsheets or memory
- Fee estimates require looking up multiple county-specific rates

## Key Features

### Status Updates
AI generates plain-language updates from checklist progress, emails them to all parties

### Title Analysis
Parses title commitments, flags exceptions, identifies potential issues

### CD Reviewer
AI review of closing disclosures against TRID tolerances. Cure amounts are AI estimates to verify against the LE and CD.

### Wire Fraud Screening
AI red flags, ABA routing checksum, and comparison to wires you confirmed by phone, plus a drafted callback request. It does not check account ownership with any outside source; always confirm by phone.

### HOA Reviewer
Extracts key terms, fees, and restrictions from HOA documents

### Fee Estimate
County-specific recording fees, transfer taxes, title insurance premiums

### Tax Proration
Calculates buyer/seller tax responsibilities with per-diem precision

### Closing Agent
Runs on demand when you click Run Agent on a matter: updates the checklist, drafts the status update, surfaces blockers. Not scheduled.

### Client Portal
Shareable read-only checklist view for buyers and sellers
